# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to treat others with respect, welcome constructive disagreement, and provide actionable feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove unacceptable material, warn participants, or restrict participation. Report concerns through a private channel available on the repository owner's profile; avoid posting sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers should handle reports fairly, keep sensitive information confidential, and avoid retaliation.
